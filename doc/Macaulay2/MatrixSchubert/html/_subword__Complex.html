subwordComplex -- to find the subword complex associated to w

  • Usage:
    subwordComplex w
  • Inputs:
    • w, a list,
  • Outputs:
    • an abstract simplicial complex,

Description

Given a permutation in 1-line notation, compute the subword complex associated to w (that is, the Stanley-Reisner complex of antiDiagInit).

i1 : subwordComplex({2,5,4,1,3})
