findRootPower -- Find the order of the root in the coefficients of polynomial ring over a cyclotomic field

  • Usage:
    findRootPower R
  • Inputs:
    • R, a polynomial ring over a cyclotomic field
  • Outputs:
    • i, The order of the adjoined root of unity, or 2 if the coefficient field is QQ
