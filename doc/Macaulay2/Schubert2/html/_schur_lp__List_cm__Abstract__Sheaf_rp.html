schur(List,AbstractSheaf) -- apply a Schur functor to an abstract sheaf

  • Function: schur
  • Usage:
    schur(p,E)
    schur_p E
  • Inputs:
    • p, a list, a partition, specified by a descending list of non-negative integers
    • E, an abstract sheaf,
  • Outputs:
    • an abstract sheaf, the result of applying the Schur functor corresponding to the partition $p$ to $E$
