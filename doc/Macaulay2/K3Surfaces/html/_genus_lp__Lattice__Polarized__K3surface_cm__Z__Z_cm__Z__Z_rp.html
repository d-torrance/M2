genus(LatticePolarizedK3surface,ZZ,ZZ) -- genus of a K3 surface

  • Function: genus
  • Usage:
    genus(S,a,b)
  • Inputs:
    • S, a K3 surface
    • a, an integer
    • b, an integer
  • Outputs:
    • an integer, the genus of $S$ embedded by the complete linear system $|a H + b C|$, where $H,C$ is the basis of the lattice associated to $S$
