skeleton(ZZ,CellComplex) -- computes the $r$-skeleton of a cell complex

  • Function: skeleton
  • Usage:
    skeleton(ZZ,CellComplex)
  • Inputs:
    • r, an integer, the dimension of the skeleton
    • C, a cell complex, which to take the skeleton of
  • Outputs:
    • a cell complex, the $r$-skeleton of the cell complex $C$

Description

The $r$-skeleton of a cell complex is the union of its cells whose dimension is at most $r$.
