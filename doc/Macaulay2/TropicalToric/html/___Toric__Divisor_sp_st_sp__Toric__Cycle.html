ToricDivisor * ToricCycle -- intersection product of a ToricDivisor and ToricCycle

  • Operator: *
  • Usage:
    D*C
    C*D
  • Inputs:
    • D, a toric divisor,
    • C, a toric cycle,

Description

Computes the intersection product of a ToricDivisor with a ToricCycle.
