isMember(RingElement,CoincidentRootLocus) -- test membership in a coincident root locus

  • Function: isMember
  • Usage:
    member(F,X)
  • Inputs:
    • F, a ring element, a binary form
    • X, a coincident root locus
  • Outputs:
    • a Boolean value, whether $F$ belongs to $X$
