ZZdFactorizationMap ++ ZZdFactorizationMap -- direct sum of ZZ/d-graded factorization maps

  • Operator: ++
  • Usage:
    h = f ++ g
    h = directSum(f,g,...)
    h = directSum(name1 => f, name2 => g, ...)
  • Inputs:
    • f, a map of ZZ/d-graded factorizations,
    • g, a map of ZZ/d-graded factorizations,
  • Outputs:
    • h, a map of ZZ/d-graded factorizations, that is the direct sum of the input ZZ/d-graded factorization maps

Description

The direct sum of two ZZ/d-graded factorization maps is a a ZZ/d-graded factorization map from the direct sum of the sources to the direct sum of the targets.

First, we define some non-trivial maps of ZZ/d-graded factorizations.
