ComplexMap ** ZZdFactorizationMap -- the map of ZZ/d-graded factorizations between tensor factorizations, obtained by first folding the relevant complex map

  • Operator: **
  • Usage:
    h = f ** g
    h = tensor(f, g)
  • Inputs:
    • f, a map of complexes, or a map of ZZ/d-graded factorizations
    • g, a map of ZZ/d-graded factorizations, or a map of complexes
  • Outputs:
    • h, a map of ZZ/d-graded factorizations,

Description

The maps $f : C \to D$ and $g : E \to F$ of ZZ/d-graded factorizations induce the map $h = f \otimes g : C \otimes E \to D \otimes F$ defined by $c \otimes e \mapsto f(c) \otimes g(e)$.
