net(TopeField) -- display a tope field

  • Function: net
  • Usage:
    net TF
  • Inputs:
    • TF, an instance of the type TopeField,
  • Outputs:
    • a net

Description

The net of a tope field displays $n$ and the type of the tope field. See TopeField.
