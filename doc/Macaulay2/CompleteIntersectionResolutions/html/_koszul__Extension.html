koszulExtension -- creates the Koszul extension complex of a map

  • Usage:
    MM = koszulExtension(FF,BB,psi1,ff)
  • Inputs:
    • FF, a complex, resolution over S
    • BB, a complex, two-term complex BB_1-->BB_0
    • psi1, a matrix, from BB_1 to FF_0
    • ff, a matrix, regular sequence annihilating the module resolved by FF
  • Outputs:
    • MM, a complex, the mapping cone of the induced map B[-1]\otimes KK(ff) to W extending psi

Description

Implements the construction in the paper "Matrix Factorizations in Higher Codimension" by Eisenbud and Peeva.

See also

  • makeFiniteResolution -- finite resolution of a matrix factorization module M

Ways to use koszulExtension:

  • koszulExtension(Complex,Complex,Matrix,Matrix)

For the programmer

The object koszulExtension is a method function.
