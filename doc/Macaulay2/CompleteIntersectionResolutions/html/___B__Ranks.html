BRanks -- ranks of the modules B_i(d) in a matrixFactorization

  • Usage:
    br = BRanks MF
  • Inputs:
    • MF, a list, output of a matrixFactorization computation
  • Outputs:
    • br, a list, list of pairs {rank B_1(d), rank B_0(d)}
