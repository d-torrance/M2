flattening -- flattening of a multidimensional matrix

  • Usage:
    flattening(s,M)
  • Inputs:
    • M, a multidimensional matrix, an $n$-dimensional matrix
    • s, a list, a subset of {0,1,...,n-1}
  • Outputs:
    • a matrix, the flattening of M corresponding to the partition (s,{0,1,...,n-1} - s)
