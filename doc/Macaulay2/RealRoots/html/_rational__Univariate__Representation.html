rationalUnivariateRepresentation -- the rational univariate representation of a zero-dimensional ideal in a polynomial ring

  • Usage:
    rationalUnivariateRepresentation(I)
    rationalUnivariateRepresentation(S)
  • Inputs:
    • I, an ideal, a zero-dimensional ideal in a polynomial ring
    • S, a quotient ring, an Artinian ring as a quotient of a polynomial ring
  • Outputs:
    • a list, the rational univariate representation of I or the rational univariate representation of S

Description

This computes the rational univariate representation of a zero-dimensional ideal in a polynomial ring.

i1 : R = QQ[x,y]

o1 = R

o1 : PolynomialRing
 
i2 : I = ideal(x*y - 1,2*x - y + 3)

o2 = ideal (x*y - 1, 2x - y + 3)

o2 : Ideal of R
 
i3 : rationalUnivariateRepresentation(I)

              2   3        - 3Z + 15  6Z + 21
o3 = (x + y, Z  - -Z - 9, {---------, -------})
                  2          4Z - 3    4Z - 3

o3 : Sequence
 
i4 : rationalUnivariateRepresentation(R/I)

              2   3        - 3Z + 15  6Z + 21
o4 = (x + y, Z  - -Z - 9, {---------, -------})
                  2          4Z - 3    4Z - 3

o4 : Sequence
