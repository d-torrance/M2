minMaxResolution -- Min and max conceivable Betti tables for generic points

  • Usage:
    minMaxResolution(r,n)
  • Inputs:
    • r, an integer, ambient dimension
    • n, an integer, number of points

Description

prints betti tables corresponding to the minimal resolution conjecture and to the lex ideal with the same Hilbert function

i1 : minMaxResolution(3,5)
min
       0 1 2 3
total: 1 5 5 1
    0: 1 . . .
    1: . 5 5 .
    2: . . . 1
max
       0 1 2 3
total: 1 6 8 3
    0: 1 . . .
    1: . 5 6 2
    2: . 1 2 1

See also

  • expectedBetti -- The betti table of r points in Pn according to the minimal resolution conjecture

For the programmer

The object minMaxResolution is a function closure.
