normalize -- normalize a Complex over QQ or RR

  • Usage:
    B = normalize C
  • Inputs:
    • C, a complex, over RR or QQ
  • Outputs:
    • B, a complex, an isomorphic Complex over QQ or RR

Description

We divide each matrix by its entry of maximal absolute value, to obtain a complex with entries of absolute size $\le 1$.
