variety(Ring) -- the variety previously associated to a given ring

  • Function: variety
  • Usage:
    variety S
  • Inputs:
    • S, a ring, the intersection ring of a variety $X$, say, or the homogeneous (Cox) ring of a normal toric variety, or another ring that has been associated to a variety, or an element in such a ring
  • Outputs:
    • a variety, $X$, the variety associated with S

For package developers: All this function does is to look up the symbol variety in S. This is currently used in two packages, but can be used in other settings, if desired.
