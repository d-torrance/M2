CoherentSheaf ^** ZZ -- tensor power

  • Operator: ^**
  • Usage:
    M^**i
  • Inputs:
    • M, a coherent sheaf
    • i, an integer
  • Outputs:
    • a coherent sheaf, the i-th tensor power of M
