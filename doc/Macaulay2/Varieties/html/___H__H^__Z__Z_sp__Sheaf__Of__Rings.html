HH^ZZ SheafOfRings -- cohomology of a sheaf of rings on a projective variety

  • Function: cohomology
  • Usage:
    HH^d(OX)
  • Inputs:
    • i, an integer,
    • OX, a sheaf of rings, on a projective variety $X$
  • Optional inputs:
    • Degree => ..., default value 0,
  • Outputs:
    • a module, representing a vector space over the coefficient field of $X$

Description

The command computes the $i$-th cohomology group of $OX$ as a vector space over the coefficient field of $X$.

i1 : Cubic = Proj QQ[x_0..x_2]/ideal(x_0^3+x_1^3+x_2^3)

o1 = Cubic

o1 : ProjectiveVariety
 
i2 : HH^1(OO_Cubic)

       1
o2 = QQ

o2 : QQ-module, free
