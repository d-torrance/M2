annihilator(CoherentSheaf) -- the annihilator ideal

  • Function: annihilator
  • Usage:
    ann F
    annihilator F
  • Inputs:
    • F, a coherent sheaf,
  • Optional inputs:
    • Strategy => a symbol, default value null, either Quotient or Intersection; see annihilator(...,Strategy=>...)
  • Outputs:
    • an ideal, the annihilator ideal

Description

The annihilator of a sheaf of modules $\mathcal F$ is the ideal corresponding to the kernel of the map of sheaves $$ \mathcal O_X \to \mathcal Hom_{\mathcal O_X}(\mathcal F, \mathcal F).b $$

You may use ann as a synonym for annihilator.
