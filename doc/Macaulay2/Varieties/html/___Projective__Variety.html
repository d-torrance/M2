ProjectiveVariety -- the class of all projective varieties

Functions and methods returning a projective variety:

  • ambient(ProjectiveVariety) -- see ambient(AffineVariety) -- the ambient affine or projective space of an embedded variety
  • Proj -- make a projective variety
  • singularLocus(ProjectiveVariety) -- see singularLocus(AffineVariety) -- the locus of singular points in a variety

Methods that use a projective variety:

  • canonicalBundle(ProjectiveVariety) -- see canonicalBundle -- the canonical bundle of a projective variety
  • char(ProjectiveVariety) -- see char(AffineVariety) -- the characteristic of the coordinate ring of a variety
  • cotangentSheaf(ProjectiveVariety) -- see cotangentSheaf -- cotangent sheaf of a projective variety
  • cotangentSheaf(ZZ,ProjectiveVariety) -- exterior powers of the cotangent sheaf of a projective variety
  • degree(ProjectiveVariety)
  • dim(ProjectiveVariety) -- dimension of the projective variety
  • euler(ProjectiveVariety) -- topological Euler characteristic of a (smooth) projective variety
  • genera(ProjectiveVariety)
  • genus(ProjectiveVariety)
  • hh(Sequence,ProjectiveVariety) -- see hh -- Hodge numbers of a smooth projective variety
  • HH^ZZ(ProjectiveVariety,SumOfTwists) -- see HH^ZZ SumOfTwists -- coherent sheaf cohomology module
  • HH^ZZ(ProjectiveVariety,CoherentSheaf) -- cohomology of a coherent sheaf on a projective variety
  • HH^ZZ(ProjectiveVariety,SheafMap) -- general cohomology functor
  • hilbertPolynomial(ProjectiveVariety) -- compute the Hilbert polynomial of the projective variety
  • idealSheaf(ProjectiveVariety) -- see idealSheaf -- ideal sheaf of a projective variety
  • isProjective(ProjectiveVariety) -- see isProjective -- whether a variety is projective
  • tangentSheaf(ProjectiveVariety) -- see tangentSheaf -- tangent sheaf of a projective variety
