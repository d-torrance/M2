CoherentSheaf ^ ZZ -- direct sum

  • Operator: ^
  • Usage:
    F^n
  • Inputs:
    • F, a coherent sheaf, , or a a sheaf of rings
    • n, an integer
  • Outputs:
    • a coherent sheaf, the direct sum of n copies of F
