allGradings -- add Grading to a Complex

  • Usage:
    Fall = allGradings(F,S)
  • Inputs:
    • F, a complex, the free resolution of an ideal
    • S, a ring, a ring with a finer garding
  • Outputs:
    • Fall, a complex, a complex over S, the same as F but now with homogeneous with respect to all gradings of S

Description

Given a resolution F of an ideal, with carries additional homogeneity with respect to the finer graded ring S, we compute the grading.
