randomDeterminantalIdeal -- produces a random determinantal ideal

  • Usage:
    I = randomDeterminantalIdeal(R,M)
  • Inputs:
    • R, a ring
    • M, a matrix, of integers
  • Outputs:
    • I, an ideal, in the ring R

Description

Given a ring and a degree matrix, we produce a random determinantal ideal with forms in prescribed degree. Forms of non-positive degrees are taken as 0 to ensure minimality of presentation.
