net(Subring) -- Short summary of a subring

  • Function: net
  • Usage:
    net S
  • Inputs:
    • S, a subring,
  • Outputs:
    • a net, obtained by formatting S for printing.

Description

Displays the presentation and ambient rings of a Subring.

i1 : R = QQ[x,y];
 
i2 : S = subring{x^2,x*y^2,y^4}

o2 = QQ[p_0..p_2], subring of R

o2 : Subring
