multipleCover -- Multiple coverings of rational curves on Calabi-Yau threefolds

  • Usage:
    multipleCover(d)
  • Inputs:
    • d, an integer, the degree d of a rational curve
  • Outputs:
    • a rational number, the contribution of multiple coverings of rational curves on Calabi-Yau threefolds

Description

Computes the contribution of multiple coverings of a smooth rational curve as a Gromov-Witten invariant.
