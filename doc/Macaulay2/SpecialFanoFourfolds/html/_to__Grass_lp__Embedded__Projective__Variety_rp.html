toGrass(EmbeddedProjectiveVariety) -- embedding of an ordinary Gushel-Mukai fourfold or a del Pezzo variety into GG(1,4)

  • Function: toGrass
  • Usage:
    toGrass X
  • Inputs:
    • X, an embedded projective variety, an ordinary Gushel-Mukai fourfold, or a del Pezzo variety of dimension at least 4 (e.g., a sixfold projectively equivalent to $\mathbb{G}(1,4)\subset\mathbb{P}^9$)
  • Outputs:
    • a multi-rational map, an embedding of $X$ into the Grassmannian $\mathbb{G}(1,4)\subset\mathbb{P}^9$, Plücker embedded
