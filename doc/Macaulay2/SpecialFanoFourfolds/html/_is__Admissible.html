isAdmissible -- whether an integer is admissible (in the sense of the theory of cubic fourfolds)

  • Usage:
    isAdmissible d
  • Inputs:
    • d, an integer
  • Outputs:
    • a Boolean value, whether d is admissible, i.e., it is an even integer d>6 which is not divisible by 4, 9 or any odd prime congruent to 2 modulo 3

Description

i1 : select(150,isAdmissible)

o1 = {14, 26, 38, 42, 62, 74, 78, 86, 98, 114, 122, 134, 146}

o1 : List

See also

  • isAdmissibleGM -- whether an integer is admissible (in the sense of the theory of GM fourfolds)

Ways to use isAdmissible:

  • isAdmissible(CubicFourfold)
  • isAdmissible(ZZ)
