surfaces -- get the special surfaces contained in the cubic fourfold

  • Usage:
    surfaces X
  • Inputs:
    • X, a doubly special cubic fourfold
  • Outputs:
    • the two surfaces contained in the fourfold X

Description

i1 : X = specialFourfold "DSCF-6";

o1 : ProjectiveVariety, cubic fourfold in C_8 containing a surface of degree 4 and sectional genus 0 and a plane
 
i2 : surfaces X

o2 = (surface in PP^5 cut out by 6 hypersurfaces of degree 2, surface in PP^5
     ------------------------------------------------------------------------
     cut out by 3 hypersurfaces of degree 1)

o2 : Sequence

See also

  • surface(HodgeSpecialFourfold) -- get the special surface contained in the fourfold

Ways to use surfaces:

  • surfaces(DoublySpecialCubicFourfold)

For the programmer

The object surfaces is a method function.
