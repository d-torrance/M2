suggestVariableOrder -- suggests a good variable ordering

  • Usage:
    suggestVariableOrder I
  • Inputs:
    • I, an ideal,
  • Outputs:
    • a list, variable ordering

Description

This method suggests a good variable ordering. The ordering is chosen by finding a good chordal completion of its constraint graph.

i1 : G = cartesianProduct(cycleGraph 3, pathGraph 3);
 
i2 : I = edgeIdeal G

o2 = monomialIdeal (x x , x x , x x , x x , x x , x x , x x , x x , x x ,
                     1 2   1 3   1 4   3 4   2 5   2 6   3 6   2 7   4 7 
     ------------------------------------------------------------------------
     x x , x x , x x , x x , x x , x x )
      6 7   5 8   6 8   5 9   7 9   8 9

o2 : MonomialIdeal of QQ[x ..x ]
                          1   9
 
i3 : X = suggestVariableOrder I

o3 = {x , x , x , x , x , x , x , x , x }
       1   3   4   2   6   5   7   8   9
