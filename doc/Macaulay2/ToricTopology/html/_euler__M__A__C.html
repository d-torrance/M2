eulerMAC -- compute the Euler characteristic of a moment-angle complex

  • Usage:
    eulerMAC(Z)
  • Inputs:
    • Z, a moment-angle complex,
  • Outputs:
    • an integer, the Euler characteristic of the moment-angle complex

Description

This method computes the Euler characteristic of moment-angle complexes.

The Euler characteristic of a moment-angle complex is $0$ if the underlying simplicial complex is not a full simplex.
