log -- logarithm function

  • Usage:
    log x
    log(b,x)
    log_b x
    log I
    log(b,I)
    log_b I
    log(J,x)
    log_J x
    log(J,I)
    log_J I
  • Inputs:
    • x, a real number or a complex number,
    • b, a real number, the base for the logarithm
    • I, a real interval or a complex interval,
    • J, a real interval, an interval of bases for the logarithm
  • Outputs:
    • a real number or a complex number, the logarithm of x
    • a real interval or a complex interval, an interval containing the logarithms of all the points of I
    • a real number, a complex number, a real interval, or a complex interval, an interval containing the logarithms for bases in J
