rowSwap -- interchange rows

  • Usage:
    rowSwap(m,i,j)
  • Inputs:
    • m, a mutable matrix
    • i, an integer
    • j, an integer
  • Consequences:
    • Interchanges the i th and j th rows of m

Description

i1 : m = mutableMatrix matrix{{1,2,3},{4,5,6}}

o1 = | 1 2 3 |
     | 4 5 6 |

o1 : MutableMatrix
 
i2 : rowSwap(m,0,1)

o2 = | 4 5 6 |
     | 1 2 3 |

o2 : MutableMatrix
 
i3 : m

o3 = | 4 5 6 |
     | 1 2 3 |

o3 : MutableMatrix
