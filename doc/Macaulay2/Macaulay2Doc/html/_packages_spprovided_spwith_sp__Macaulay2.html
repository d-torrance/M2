packages provided with Macaulay2

Here is a list of the packages that are distributed with Macaulay2. The ones that have been refereed and published are marked with a star.

Algebraic Geometry

  • Cremona -- rational maps between projective varieties
  • HyperplaneArrangements -- manipulating finite sets of hyperplanes
  • IncidenceCorrespondenceCohomology -- Cohomology on the incidence correspondence, bundle of principal parts, and Lefschetz properties
  • Jets -- compute jets of various algebraic, geometric and combinatorial objects
  • K3Surfaces -- Explicit constructions of K3 surfaces
  • NumericalSemigroups -- Compute the Apery set and invariants of a numerical semigroup ring
  • PencilsOfQuadrics -- Clifford Algebra of a pencil of quadratic forms
  • SpecialFanoFourfolds -- Hodge-special fourfolds
  • Varieties -- routines for working with affine and projective varieties and coherent sheaves on them
  • WhitneyStratifications -- Compute Whitney Stratifications

Algebraic Number Theory

  • Cyclotomic -- cyclotomic fields
  • EllipticCurves -- group law on elliptic curves (and counting points with brute force methods)
  • LLLBases -- Lenstra-Lenstra-Lovasz bases
  • Padic -- p-adic numbers

Algebraic Statistics

  • AllMarkovBases -- computing all minimal Markov bases of a configuration matrix
  • GraphicalModels -- discrete and Gaussian graphical models
  • GraphicalModelsMLE -- maximum likelihood estimates for graphical statistical models
  • Markov -- Markov ideals arising from Bayesian networks in statistics
  • MultigradedImplicitization -- solving implicitization problems using multigradings
  • Probability -- basic probability functions
  • StatGraphs -- Graphs specific for algebraic statistics

Applied Algebraic Geometry

  • AlgebraicSplines -- splines on simplicial complexes, polytopal complexes, and graphs
  • EliminationTemplates -- Elimination Templates
  • EuclideanDistanceDegree -- produce critical equations and compute ED degrees
  • IntegerProgramming -- solving integer programs with Gröbner bases
  • Oscillators -- code to analyze graph oscillators
  • PathSignatures -- working with algebraic path signatures
  • PhylogeneticTrees -- invariants for group-based phylogenetic models
  • ReactionNetworks -- reaction networks

Coding Theory

  • CodingTheory -- tools for coding theory
  • NeuralIdeals -- canonical forms of neural ideals

Combinatorial Commutative Algebra

  • AbstractSimplicialComplexes -- Abstract Simplicial Complexes
  • MacaulayPosets -- Macaulay posets
  • MonomialOrbits -- Orbit representatives of monomial ideals
  • NeuralIdeals -- canonical forms of neural ideals
  • PolyominoIdeals -- Collections of cells and binomial ideals
  • ResolutionsOfStanleyReisnerRings -- Comparing resolutions of Stanley-Reisner rings and computing various systems of parameters
  • SimplicialComplexes -- exploring abstract simplicial complexes within commutative algebra
  • SimplicialDecomposability -- various decomposability routines for simplicial complexes
  • SimplicialPosets -- constructing Stanley simplicial poset rings
  • SRdeformations -- deformations of Stanley-Reisner rings and related computations

Combinatorics

  • MatrixSchubert -- functions for investigating ASM and matrix Schubert varieties

  • SymmetricPolynomials -- symmetric polynomials
  • Tableaux -- constructing Young tableaux
  • Triangulations -- triangulations of polyhedra and point sets

  • Hadamard -- Hadamard products of projective subvarieties
  • HigherCIOperators -- higher CI operators
