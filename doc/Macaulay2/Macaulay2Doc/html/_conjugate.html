conjugate -- complex conjugate

  • Usage:
    conjugate z
  • Inputs:
    • z
  • Outputs:
    • a complex number, the complex conjugate of z
    • a complex interval, a complex interval containing all complex conjugate of points of z

Description

i1 : conjugate(1+2.5*ii)

o1 = 1-2.5*ii

o1 : CC (of precision 53)
 
i2 : conjugate 3

o2 = 3
 
i3 : conjugate interval(1+2*ii,3+4*ii)

o3 = [1,3]+[-4,-2]*ii

o3 : CCi (of precision 53)

Ways to use conjugate:

  • conjugate(CC)
  • conjugate(CCi)
  • conjugate(Constant)
  • conjugate(Number)
  • conjugate(Partition) -- conjugate a partition

For the programmer

The object conjugate is a method function.
