coth -- hyperbolic cotangent

  • Usage:
    coth x
    coth I
  • Inputs:
    • x, a real number
    • x, a complex number
    • I, a real interval
    • I, a complex interval
  • Outputs:
    • a real number, the hyperbolic cotangent of x
    • a real interval, an interval containing the hyperbolic cotangents of the points of I
    • a complex interval, a complex interval containing the hyperbolic cotangents of the points of I

Description

i1 : coth(pi/3)

o1 = 1.280878071045045

o1 : RR (of precision 53)

See Hyperbolic function.

Ways to use coth:

  • coth(InexactNumber)

For the programmer

The object coth is a method function.
