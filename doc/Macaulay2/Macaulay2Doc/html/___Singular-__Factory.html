Singular-Factory

With the kind permission of the authors of Singular, G.-M. Greuel, R. Stobbe, G. Pfister, H. Schoenemann, and J. Schmidt, University of Kaiserslautern, Macaulay2 incorporates Singular-Factory, version 4.3.2, a free library of polynomial routines that provides for factorization of polynomials. It is part of Singular, whose home page is https://www.singular.uni-kl.de/.

The following important routines call upon it:
  • factor -- factor a ring element or a ZZ-module
  • gcd -- greatest common divisor
  • minimalPrimes -- minimal primes of an ideal
  • irreducibleCharacteristicSeries -- irreducible characteristic series of an ideal
