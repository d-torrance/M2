tensor(Matrix,Matrix) -- tensor product

  • Function: tensor
  • Usage:
    f ** g
    tensor(f, g)
  • Inputs:
    • f, a matrix,
    • g, a matrix,
  • Outputs:
    • a matrix, the tensor product of maps $f$ and $g$

Description

Other names for the tensor product include: the outer product, or the Kronecker product of two matrices.

i1 : R = ZZ[a..d];
 
i2 : f = matrix {{a,b}}

o2 = | a b |

             1      2
o2 : Matrix R  <-- R
 
i3 : g = transpose matrix {{c,d}}

o3 = {-1} | c |
     {-1} | d |

             2      1
o3 : Matrix R  <-- R
 
i4 : f ** g

o4 = {-1} | ac bc |
     {-1} | ad bd |

             2      2
o4 : Matrix R  <-- R
