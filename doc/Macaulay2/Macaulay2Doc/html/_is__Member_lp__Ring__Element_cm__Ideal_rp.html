isMember(RingElement,Ideal) -- test membership in an ideal

  • Function: isMember
  • Usage:
    isMember(r,I)
  • Inputs:
    • r, a ring element or a number,
    • I, an ideal,
  • Outputs:
    • a Boolean value, whether r is in the ideal I;
