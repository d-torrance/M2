gcd -- greatest common divisor

  • Usage:
    gcd(x,y,...)
  • Inputs:
    • x, an integer
    • , or
    • a rational number
    • , or
    • a ring element
  • Outputs:
    • an integer, , or a rational number, or a ring element, the greatest common divisor of the arguments

Description

i1 : gcd(12,8,48)

o1 = 4
 
i2 : R = QQ[x,y,z];
 
i3 : gcd(x^2*y,x*y^3^6)

o3 = x*y

o3 : R
 
i4 : gcd(x^36-1,x^24-1)

      12
o4 = x   - 1

o4 : R
