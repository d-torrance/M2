ZZ & ZZ -- logical and

  • Operator: &
  • Usage:
    m & n
  • Inputs:
    • m, an integer
    • n, an integer
  • Outputs:
    • an integer, obtained from the bits of the integers m and n by logical 'and'.

Description

i1 : (2^15 + 2^13 + 2^42) & (2^15 + 2^23 + 2^42) == 2^15 + 2^42

o1 = true
