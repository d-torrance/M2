gfanTropicalHyperSurface -- the tropical hypersurface of a principal ideal

  • Usage:
    F = gfanTropicalHyperSurface(f)
  • Inputs:
    • f, a ring element, a polynomial
  • Outputs:
    • F, an instance of the type Fan, the tropical hypersurface of the ideal generated by f

Description

This method computes the tropical hypersurface of a principal ideal.
