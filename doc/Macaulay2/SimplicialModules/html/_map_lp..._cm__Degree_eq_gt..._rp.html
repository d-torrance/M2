map(...,Degree=>...) -- specify the degree of a map of simplicial modules

Description

When constructing a map of simplicial modules, this option specifies the degree $d$ of the resulting map. A map of degree $d$ consists of component maps $f_i : C_i \to D_{i+d}$. By default, the degree is 0.
