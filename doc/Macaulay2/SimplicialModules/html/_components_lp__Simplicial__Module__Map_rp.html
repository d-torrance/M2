components(SimplicialModuleMap) -- list the components of a direct sum

  • Function: components
  • Usage:
    components f
  • Inputs:
    • f, a Map of Simplicial Modules,
  • Outputs:
    • a list, the component maps of a direct sum of maps of simplicial modules
