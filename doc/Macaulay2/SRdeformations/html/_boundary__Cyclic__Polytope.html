boundaryCyclicPolytope -- The boundary complex of a cyclic polytope.

  • Usage:
    boundaryCyclicPolytope(d,R)
  • Inputs:
    • d, an integer, positive
    • R, a polynomial ring,
  • Outputs:
    • an embedded complex,

Description

Returns the boundary complex of a cyclic polytope of dimension d inside the standard simplex of the variables of R. So the vertices are those of the standard simplex.

i1 : R=QQ[x_0..x_5]

o1 = R
