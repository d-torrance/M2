canonicalCurve -- Compute a random canonical curve of genus less or equal to 14

  • Usage:
    I=(random canonicalCurve)(g,S)
  • Inputs:
    • g, an integer, the genus
    • R, a polynomial ring, homogeneous coordinate ring of $\PP^{ g-1}$
  • Outputs:
    • I, an ideal, of a canonical curve $C$ of genus $g$

Description

Compute a random canonical curve of genus $g \le{} 14$, based on the proofs of unirationality of $M_g$ by Severi, Sernesi, Chang-Ran and Verra.

i1 : setRandomSeed "alpha";
 -- setting random seed to 10206284518
 
i2 : g=14;
 
i3 : FF=ZZ/10007;
 
i4 : R=FF[x_0..x_(g-1)];
 
i5 : time betti(I=(random canonicalCurve)(g,R))
 -- used 8.65568s (cpu); 6.76278s (thread); 0s (gc)

o5 = total: 1 66

o5 : BettiTally
 
i6 : genus I == g and degree I ==2*g-2
