isCI(Ideal) -- whether the ring is complete intersection

  • Function: isCI
  • Usage:
    isCI R or isCI I
  • Inputs:
    • I, an ideal, of a polynomial algebra contained in the irrelevant maximal ideal
  • Outputs:
    • a Boolean value, whether the local ring obtained by localizing the quotient by I at the irrelevant maximal ideal is complete intersection

Description

Checks if the local ring obtained by localizing the quotient by I at the irrelevant maximal ideal is complete intersection.

i1 : Q = QQ[x,y,z];
 
i2 : isCI (ideal(x^2,x*y,y*z,z^2))

o2 = false
 
i3 : isCI (ideal(x^2,y^2))

o3 = true
