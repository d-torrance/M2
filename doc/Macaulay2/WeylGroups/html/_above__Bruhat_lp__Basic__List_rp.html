aboveBruhat(BasicList) -- The Weyl group elements just under the ones in the list for the Bruhat order

  • Function: aboveBruhat
  • Usage:
    aboveBruhat(B)
  • Inputs:
    • B, a basic list, of WeylGroupElement
  • Outputs:
    • a list, of elements greater than the ones in B and of length one more together with the root of the reflection composed with (on the right) to obtain the element
