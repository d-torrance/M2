makeKGroupMackeyFunctor -- constructs the $K$-group Mackey functor

  • Usage:
    makeKGroupMackeyFunctor(p,q,n)
  • Inputs:
    • p, an integer, a prime number $p$
    • q, an integer, a prime power $q$
    • n, an integer, a positive integer $n$
  • Outputs:
    • a C_p Mackey Functor, the fixed point $C_p$-Mackey functor of the algebraic $K$-group $K_{2n-1}(\mathbb{F}_{q^p})$.
