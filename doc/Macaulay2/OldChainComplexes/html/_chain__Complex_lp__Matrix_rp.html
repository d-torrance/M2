chainComplex(Matrix) -- make a small chain complex

  • Function: chainComplex
  • Usage:
    chainComplex f
  • Inputs:
    • f, a matrix
  • Outputs:
    • a chain complex, a chain complex with the map f serving as the differential C.dd_1

Description

i1 : R = QQ[x,y];
