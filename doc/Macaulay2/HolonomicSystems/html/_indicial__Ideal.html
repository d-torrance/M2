indicialIdeal -- the image in the thetaRing of an indicial ideal in a Weyl algebra

  • Usage:
    indicialIdeal(I,w)
  • Inputs:
    • I, an ideal, in a WeylAlgebra that is torus-fixed
    • w, a list, (generic) weights for $I$, of length half the number of variables in the Weyl algebra
  • Outputs:
    • an ideal, that results from intersecting with the thetaRing of D with the result of extending the ideal in_(-w,w)(I) with the rational function field in the Weyl algebra variables, as in [SST, Theorem 2.3.9] to I.

Description

This computes the distraction of a torus fixed D-ideal [SST, Corollary 2.3.5]. This is necessary to compute indicial ideals [SST, Theorem 2.3.9, Corollary 2.3.5]; the roots of the indicial ideals are the exponents of the starting terms of canonical series solutions of regular holonomic D-ideals.
