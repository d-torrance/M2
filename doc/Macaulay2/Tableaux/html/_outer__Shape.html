outerShape -- the outer shape of a (skew) Young tableau

  • Usage:
    mu = outerShape T
  • Inputs:
    • T, an instance of the type YoungTableau, a Young tableau of shape $\lambda/\mu$.
  • Outputs:
    • lam, an instance of the type Partition, the outer shape, $\lambda, of T.
