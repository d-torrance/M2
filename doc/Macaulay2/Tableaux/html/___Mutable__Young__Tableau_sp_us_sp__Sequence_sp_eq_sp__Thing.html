MutableYoungTableau _ Sequence = Thing -- change an entry of a mutable Young tableau

  • Operator: _
  • Usage:
    T_thePosition = k
  • Inputs:
    • T, an instance of the type MutableYoungTableau, a mutable tableau.
    • thePosition, a sequence, a pair (i,j) of integers.
    • k, a thing, the new entry in position (i,j).
  • Outputs:
    • T, an instance of the type MutableYoungTableau, a mutable tableau with entry k in position (i,j).
