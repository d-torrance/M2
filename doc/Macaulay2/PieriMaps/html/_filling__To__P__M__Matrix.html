fillingToPMMatrix -- QQ matrix of the Filling-to-PM change of basis on a Schur module

  • Usage:
    fillingToPMMatrix(mu, n)
  • Inputs:
    • mu, a list, a partition
    • n, an integer
  • Outputs:
    • a matrix, a $\mathbb{Q}$ matrix; rows = PM tableaux of $\mu$, cols = Fillings of $\mu^t$. Entry $(i, j)$ = coefficient of PM tableau $i$ in fillingToPM(filling_j).

Description

Inverse direction of pmToFillingMatrix; same equivariant iso, transposed convention.
