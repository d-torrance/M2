makeALDifferential -- "makes the differential used in ciOperatorResolution"

  • Usage:
    dj = makeALDifferential(j, A, L, u)
  • Inputs:
    • j, an integer, which differential
    • A, a complex, lift of resolution from complete intersection S-->R
    • L, a complex, Koszul complex resolving R over S
    • u, a hash table, output of higherCIOperators
  • Outputs:
    • dj, a matrix, j-th differential of the ciOperatorResolution AL

See also

  • ciOperatorResolution -- "lift resolution from complete intersection using higher ci-operators"
  • higherCIOperators -- "creates the HashTable of higher CI operators on a lifted resolution"

Ways to use makeALDifferential:

  • makeALDifferential(ZZ,Complex,Complex,HashTable)

For the programmer

The object makeALDifferential is a method function.
