point(MultiprojectiveVariety) -- pick a random rational point on a multi-projective variety

  • Function: point
  • Usage:
    point X
  • Inputs:
    • X, a multi-projective variety, defined over a finite field
  • Outputs:
    • a multi-projective variety, a random rational point on $X$
