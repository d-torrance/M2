tangentSpace -- tangent space to a projective variety at a point

  • Usage:
    tangentSpace(X,p)
    tangentSpace(p,X)
  • Inputs:
    • X, an embedded projective variety
    • p, an embedded projective variety, a point on $X$
  • Outputs:
    • an embedded projective variety, the embedded tangent space $T_p(X)$ to $X$ at the point $p$
