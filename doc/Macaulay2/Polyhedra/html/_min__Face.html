minFace -- computes the face of a Polyhedron or Cone where a weight attains its minimum

  • Usage:
    F = minFace(w,P)
    F = minFace(w,C)
  • Inputs:
    • w, a matrix, over ZZ or QQ with only one column representing a weight vector
    • P, a convex polyhedron
    • C, a convex rational cone
  • Outputs:
    • F, Depending on the input, a Cone or a Polyhedron, the face where w attains its minimum

Description

minFace computes the face of the given Polyhedron P or Cone C where w attains its minimum.
