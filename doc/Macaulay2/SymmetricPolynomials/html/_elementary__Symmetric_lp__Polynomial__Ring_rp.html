elementarySymmetric(PolynomialRing) -- elementary symmetric polynomials algebra

  • Function: elementarySymmetric
  • Usage:
    elementarySymmetric R
  • Inputs:
    • R, a polynomial ring, aPolynomialRing
  • Outputs:
