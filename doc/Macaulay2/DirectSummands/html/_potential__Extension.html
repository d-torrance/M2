potentialExtension -- suggest a field extension where splitting may become visible

  • Usage:
    potentialExtension M
  • Inputs:
    • M, a module or a coherent sheaf,
  • Outputs:
    • a ring, typically a splitting field of a sampled endomorphism

Description

This function inspects a general endomorphism of $M$ and returns a candidate field extension that may expose further splitting.

See also

  • changeBaseField -- extend scalars for modules, matrices, and coherent sheaves
  • generalEndomorphism -- construct a generic degree-zero endomorphism

Ways to use potentialExtension:

  • potentialExtension(Module)

For the programmer

The object potentialExtension is a method function.
