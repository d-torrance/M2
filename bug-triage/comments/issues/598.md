### The reported defect

`diff(t, t^-1)` is `-t^(-2)`, which is what the quotient rule gives, and `diff(t, 1_R)` is
`0`. That is the whole subject of this issue and it is right.

### The question left in #599

@gblanco92 closed that pull request after finding the patch incomplete, writing:

> This fix does not work for `contract` and Laurent series. It still outputs
> `contract(t, t^-1)` → `0`. I'm not sure what the expected output of `contract` on a
> Laurent series should be. For instance, should `contract(t, 1_R)` be equal to `0` or
> `t^-1`?

Today M2 gives `contract(t, 1_R) = t^(-1)` — the second of the two branches he named — and
`contract(t, t^-1) = t^(-2)`, consistently with it. So the case that blocked his patch now
behaves, and the convention he was unsure about has been settled in practice.

Worth being precise about *how* it was settled, though: as far as I can tell nobody stated
the choice. The behaviour changed and the question was never answered in the thread, so the
convention is recorded by the implementation rather than by a decision or by the
documentation. The four values above are here so that it is written down somewhere at least
once.

Thank you for the report and for the attempt at a fix — the analysis in #599, including
identifying that `contract` shares the code path and that the `is_invertible` check was the
relevant guard, is why the case is easy to check now.
