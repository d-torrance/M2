Resolved by a package merge, so closing.

`RandomGenus14Curves` no longer exists: @eisenbud folded it into `RandomCurves` in
dea800ed3f (2026-06-07), along with `RandomCanonicalCurves`, `RandomPlaneCurves` and
`RandomSpaceCurves`. The same commit dealt with the tests. The genus-14 construction test went from
100 random attempts over `ZZ/3` to a single attempt on a fixed seed, and the `ZZ/11` certification
test was dropped entirely — the two that carried the author's own "uses ca. 150 seconds" and "uses
ca. 130 seconds" comments.

On 1.26.06 the old invocation still works and redirects:

```
 -- warning: the 'RandomGenus14Curves' package has been merged into 'RandomCurves'.
 -- capturing check(11, "RandomCurves") -- 1.28647s elapsed
 -- capturing check(12, "RandomCurves") -- 10.56s elapsed
 -- capturing check(13, "RandomCurves") -- 12.0551s elapsed
```

Test 11 is the surviving genus-14 one, at 1.29 s against the 63.1 s and 30.9 s reported here. All
fourteen `RandomCurves` tests together take 27.07 s wall and 314 MB peak RSS, so the merged suite now
costs well under half of what the two genus-14 tests alone did.

The 314 MB figure is also relevant to the armel out-of-memory failure, since these run under
`GC_MAXIMUM_HEAP_SIZE=400M` — it is no longer obviously over the line, though I have not tested a
32-bit architecture.
