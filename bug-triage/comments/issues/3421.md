Closing as obsolete — not because the checklist was finished, but because the question it was
opened to answer is no longer open.

Apple silicon on Sonoma is now a routinely built target: the Macaulay2 Homebrew formula ships
`arm64_sonoma` bottles alongside `arm64_sequoia` and `arm64_tahoe`, so macOS 14, 15 and 26 on Apple
silicon are all covered, and the tap's `brew test-bot` is green. Sonoma is two releases back at this
point.

Two things from the thread are worth carrying rather than losing:

- The rows never ticked here were the **alternative compilers** — llvm clang and gcc14 — plus
  `check` under appleclang. The bottles establish the mainline appleclang path and say nothing about
  the other two, so if building M2 with llvm clang or gcc on Apple silicon is still wanted, that is
  a distinct question and worth its own issue.
- @dimpase's finding at the end of the thread — that the `normal/` ctest failures came down to
  `gbTrace` not being set — was fixed separately in
  [#4303](https://github.com/Macaulay2/M2/issues/4303), closed 2026-05-16.
