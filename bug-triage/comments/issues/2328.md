This no longer happens. On 1.26.06, `needsPackage "NormalToricVarieties"` followed by
`loadPackage("Macaulay2Doc", LoadDocumentation => true)` loads without complaint.

The cause looks like `347b7e98f2`, "Drop PackageExports from Macaulay2Doc", which went in with #3642
and first appeared in 1.25.05. @DanGrayson's diagnosis here was that both symbols come from
`Normaliz` and that loading `Macaulay2Doc` this way "causes *all* other packages to be loaded" —
dropping `PackageExports` is exactly what stops that.

What makes that a chain rather than a coincidence is that nothing else moved: `Normaliz.m2` still
exports `allComputations` and `grading`, `NormalToricVarieties.m2` still lists `Normaliz` under
`PackageImports`, and the guard is still in place, erroring if `Macaulay2Doc`'s private dictionary
comes out non-empty. The precondition and the check both survive; only the transitive loading is
gone.

One caveat on the attribution: dropping `PackageExports` was incidental to what #3642 was for, so
this is a reading of cause rather than a fix that was linked here.
