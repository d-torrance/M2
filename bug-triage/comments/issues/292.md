Re-scoping and retitling this, because it is the issue that already carries the open
question and its title describes something that no longer happens.

### It no longer hangs

```
i1 : R = ZZ[x_1..x_3, MonomialOrder=>{RevLex}, Global =>false ];

i2 : gens gb ideal(-2+9*x_1^3, -1+3*x_1^2)
Core/gb.m2:160:28:(1):[17]: error: Groebner bases in rings over ZZ with variables
                                   less than zero are not yet supported
```

That is the error @mikestillman proposed in this very thread — *"Should we give an error now
(local term orders in polynomial rings over ZZ not yet supported?)"* — with @jakobkroeker
agreeing (*"I'm fine with an error for now (release 1.8)"*) and @DanGrayson noting that
nobody had asked for the capability. It was specified as #294 the next day, closed completed
on 8 June 2015 for the front-end checks, and given its engine-side guard in 2017 by
[`e552ee18be`](https://github.com/Macaulay2/M2/commit/e552ee18be), whose message is
*"disallow for now: local gb over ZZ"*.

### What is still open, and why it lives here

The capability itself, and this thread is where the reason is written down:

> It turns out that local ring computations over ZZ are not implemented: the minimalization
> routine called does not work in this case. **Eventually, we should add this.**

The Gröbner basis computation is not the obstacle — Mike notes above that it *"completes the
GB internally, and then hangs during minimalization"*. It is the minimalization step that
does not handle this case. Nothing since has changed that; the 2017 commit says "for now",
and the two front-end conditions at
[`m2/gb.m2:122-125`](https://github.com/Macaulay2/M2/blob/development/M2/Macaulay2/m2/gb.m2#L122-L125)
still read as temporary.

So this issue is retitled from "gb computation over ZZ hangs?" to name what it now tracks,
and retyped from Bug to Feature.
