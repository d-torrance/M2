Closing as fixed. @DanGrayson's diagnosis above — *"it expects to find the module `C_1` in the
chain complex `C`, but, being zero, it's not actually there"* — is what got fixed, by
@eisenbud in **#729**, commit `4909c6c145` ("resolved issue #725 and added a TEST
toChainComplexExtras"), merged 2018-01-15, a week after the report and with a regression test.
The issue was simply never closed.

On 1.26.06-40-gd8e86d689d all four calls succeed, and the results are right rather than merely
non-erroring. For `C = QQ^1[0]`, concentrated in degree 0:

| call | span | modules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `prependZeroMap C` | `-1 .. 0` | `QQ^0`, `QQ^1` |
| `appendZeroMap C` | `0 .. 1` | `QQ^1`, `QQ^0` |

so the zero module lands one step below and one step above the concentration respectively,
which is what the names promise. `QQ^1[-1]` behaves the same way, shifted.

Thanks @foxjwill for the report and @eisenbud for the fix.
