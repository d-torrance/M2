Confirmed, still, on 1.26.06 — and the option is less broken than discarded. `galois.m2:174`
switches to the `FlintBig` representation and overwrites the primitive element with `S_0`, the
ring's own variable, whenever the element in hand is anything else:

```m2
if d >= opts.SizeLimit or primitiveElement != S_0 then (
    typ = "FlintBig";
    primitiveElement = S_0; -- Possibly NOT the primitive element in this case!!
```

In your example `d` is 48 and `SizeLimit` defaults to 10000, so it is the second condition that
fires. That throws away both what `FindOne` searched for and what you supplied, and
`F.PrimitiveElement` is set from the result — which is why both examples print `z`.

Worth saying that your fields are otherwise fine. The substitution is deliberate: for `FlintBig`
the engine needs to be told where `S_0` goes rather than where a primitive element goes, which is
what `ringmap.m2:122` uses it for. Arithmetic and ring maps out of these fields are sound — 200
random pairs through `map(F[t], F)` gave no additive or multiplicative failures. What is wrong is
the option and the `PrimitiveElement` attribute, not the field itself.

One thing for whoever picks this up: the manual demonstrates the broken usage. `ov_rings.m2:646`
says "you may also provide your own choice of primitive element", adds that elements are stored
internally as powers of it, and gives `GF(ZZ/2[T]/(T^9+T+1), PrimitiveElement => T^3+1)` — where
`T^3+1` is primitive, `T` is not, 511 is under the size limit, and `k.PrimitiveElement` comes back
`T`.
