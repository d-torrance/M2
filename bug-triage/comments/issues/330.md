Reproduces byte-identically on 1.26.06-40, and @jmokland's one-line remark above turns out
to be the whole answer — confirmed with numbers here for the first time. **Not a defect in
the computation; a gap between three separately-documented conventions.** Re-scoping to
documentation rather than closing, because the missing sentence is concrete.

### The numbers

```m2
X = projectiveBundle 4;
run = F -> (
    Y := projectiveBundle F;  p := Y.StructureMap;  K := chern(1, OO_Y(1));
    (segre F, p_*(1+K+K^2+K^3+K^4+K^5+K^6)));
```

| | `segre F` | pushforward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rank 2, `OO_X(2) ++ OO_X(3)` | `1+5H+19H+65H+211H` | `1+5H+19H+65H+211H` — agree |
| rank 3, `OO_X(2) ++ OO_X(3) ++ OO_X(4)` | `1+9H+55H+285H+1351H` | `1+18H+217H+2190H+19981H` — disagree |

and with `projectiveBundle'` in place of `projectiveBundle`, the rank-3 pushforward is
`1+9H+55H+285H+1351H` — exactly `segre F`.

So the rank-2 case agreeing was the coincidence, not the rank-3 case disagreeing.

### The three conventions, each documented, whose interaction is not

All of these are stated plainly in `Schubert2/doc.m2`, on their own pages:

- **`projectiveBundle`** — *"the projective bundle of rank 1 sub-bundles of the abstract
  sheaf $F$."*
- **`projectiveBundle'`** — *"the projective bundle of rank 1 quotient bundles of the
  abstract sheaf $F$."*
- **`segre A`** — *"the total Segre class of `A`, defined as the reciprocal of the Chern
  class of the **dual** of `A`. (In a future version, the dual may be omitted, and an
  alternative function `segre'` may be introduced.)"*

Each page is clear about itself, and the two `projectiveBundle` pages cross-reference each
other. What no page says is **which pairing satisfies the classical Segre-class formula** —
and the measurement above says it is `segre` with `projectiveBundle'`, not with
`projectiveBundle`. That is the sentence Aluffi needed, and the page he was following was
the `projectiveBundle` documentation.

Worth noting that `segre` carries its own dual and its own warning that the convention may
change, so the pairing is not something a reader can safely derive from the two
`projectiveBundle` pages alone.

### What would settle this

A sentence on the `(segre,AbstractSheaf)` page, and probably on `projectiveBundle` as well,
naming the variant for which
$s(F) = p_*\left(\sum_i c_1(\mathcal{O}(1))^i\right)$
holds as written. The example above is ready-made for it, since it shows the identity
holding, failing, and holding again on one bundle.

I have deliberately not labelled either convention "Fulton-style" or "Grothendieck-style"
here. `segre`'s built-in dual interacts with the sub/quotient choice, and getting that
attribution right is a job for someone who works in this area — the measurement above is
convention-free and stands on its own.
