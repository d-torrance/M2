An inventory of the proposed scheme against what exists on 1.26.06-40, since this has been
open eleven years and part of it has quietly happened elsewhere.

| --- | --- |

| `cokernelMap` | — |

| `coimageMap` | — |
| `coverMap` | **defined** (and Dan already noted it existed in 2015) |

So nothing has been added to Core since the scheme was proposed.

Two of the names do exist, but in packages rather than Core, and were presumably arrived at
independently: `doubleDualMap` (about 19 occurrences) and `pruneMap` (about 4), across
`AnalyzeSheafOnP1`, `CompleteIntersectionResolutions`, `DGAlgebras`, `Kronecker` and
`PrimaryDecomposition/Eisenbud-Huneke-Vasconcelos`. That is worth knowing in both
directions: it is evidence the names are wanted, and it means any Core implementation has
existing callers to reconcile with rather than a clean field.

On @mikestillman's note that this was planned for `Complexes` in 1.17 — that does not
appear to have landed. `Complexes` uses `inducedMap` (42 occurrences) and none of the names
above.

`inducedMap` being the workhorse throughout is probably the substantive argument for the
proposal rather than against it: the maps in the table all exist as objects, they just have
to be spelled as an `inducedMap` call with the right arguments each time, which is exactly
the friction the scheme was meant to remove.
