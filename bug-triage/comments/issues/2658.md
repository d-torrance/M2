Confirming this after four years with no reply: all three outputs still come out exactly as reported,
and your hand computation is the correct one. For rank-2 `E` with Chern roots `a, b`, `Sym^2 E` has
roots `2a`, `a+b`, `2b`, giving the `1 + 3c_1 + (2c_1^2 + 4c_2) + 4c_1c_2` that M2 returns, so your
dual is right too and the disagreement really is in `gamma_*OO_PSym2E(1)`, as you suspected.

The `'` variant is the one that behaves. For `projectiveBundle'` the pushforward of `O(1)` equals
`chern V` exactly — at rank 2, at rank 3, and for `Sym^2 E`. For `projectiveBundle` it is never
`chern dual V`, the classical answer you expected; it is always `chern(dual V ** det V)`. In your
example `det Sym^2 E = (det E)^3`, which is why the numbers look far apart rather than off by a sign.

The twist comes from how `O(1)` is chosen on a flag bundle, in
[`Schubert2.m2:781-785`](https://github.com/Macaulay2/M2/blob/development/M2/Macaulay2/packages/Schubert2.m2#L781-L785):

```m2
X.TautologicalLineBundle = OO_X(sum(1 .. #B - 1, i -> i * chern(1,B#i)));
```

`projectiveBundle V` is `flagBundle({1, rank V - 1}, V)`, so `B#1` is the rank-(n−1) *quotient* and
this makes `O(1)` into `det Q = det V ** dual S`, rather than `dual S`, the dual of the tautological
rank-1 sub. `projectiveBundle'` is `flagBundle({rank V - 1, 1}, V)`, where `B#1` is the rank-1
quotient and the same formula gives the right thing. At rank 2 the mistake is easy to miss, since
`dual V ** det V` is isomorphic to `V` there.

Probably the same root cause as #330, which you linked — that one is about these conventions being
undocumented, and this is the wrong-answer face of it.
