Closing: this was settled in the other direction, and the underlying complaint was fixed on its own.

`#pragma once` was never adopted wholesale, but it did spread — 4 headers under `e/` had it when
this was filed, 23 by mid-2026. [PR #4347](https://github.com/Macaulay2/M2/pull/4347),
*"Organizing the e directory"*, merged 2026-06-06 and described as *"the changes from the Macaulay2
workshop in Atlanta"*, removed all 23: @dimpase's commit `d84e06a657`, *"deal with `#pragma once`
— replace them by the ifndef guards"*. There are none left in `e/` today.

The second point in the original post is the one that got fixed:

> The identifiers I am currently using for these guards often start with an underscore, which is
> not always allowed by the standard in any case.

`79c7ac4fc5`, *"standartize guards in `e/**.hpp` and `e/**.h`"*, in the same PR, took
leading-underscore guard identifiers in `e/` from **208 to 8**. Five of the remaining eight are not
M2's own guards at all — `__FFLASFFPACK_config_blas_H` twice in the vendored `lapack.hpp`, and
`__cplusplus`, `_GNU_SOURCE` and `__has_feature` — and the other three are new `e/cytools/` headers
added after that pass.

So the question @DanGrayson, @moorewf, @mahrud, @antonleykin and @d-torrance were asked here has an
answer now, just not the one the issue proposed.
