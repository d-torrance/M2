Closing as `wontfix` — not because the inconvenience isn't real, but because each of the concrete
proposals here has already been settled one way or the other.

**The single-file consolidation was offered and turned down.** @DanGrayson volunteered it twice in
this thread; the reply was that it wasn't what was being asked for. That leaves it declined by the
reporter rather than ignored by the maintainers.

**Per-package changelogs are the author's call, and the practice already exists** without needing
new infrastructure. Eleven distributed packages carry version-history prose in their documentation
today, several of them in the top-level node — `A1BrouwerDegrees`, `BettiCharacters`,
`HighestWeights`, `InvariantRing`, `Jets`, `LieAlgebraRepresentations`, `NeuralIdeals`, `Pullback`,
`RunExternalM2`, `TestIdeals`, `VectorFields`. Others keep the history in source comments. An author
who wants a History page for their package can write one now.

That leaves the per-version structure of `changes.m2` itself, which has been the status quo for
years and for which no replacement has been proposed that the reporter and the maintainers agree
on. If it is worth revisiting with a specific design, a new issue carrying that design would be a
better starting point than this thread.
