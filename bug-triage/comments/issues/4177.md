[`ThreadSupervisor::getTask()`](https://github.com/Macaulay2/M2/blob/f0bb5e5caec57e8c0d190b93e88ff1f3f6f0e4e7/M2/Macaulay2/system/supervisor.cpp#L341-L357)
blocks in `pthread_cond_wait` without re-checking it. Threads already
parked in `getTask` when the limit drops therefore each take one more task before they come back
round to the test and start sleeping. That is the one full cycle.

parked in `getTask`, so they reach the test within a tick and pick up work straight away.
