Closing as `wontfix`, in the sense of "answered elsewhere" rather than "declined": this is guidance
rather than a code change, so it has gone into the
[Package Writing Style Guide](https://github.com/Macaulay2/M2/wiki/Package-Writing-Style-Guide),
which until now said nothing about mathematical notation at all. Two bullets, in its Documentation
section:

> **Mathematics:** set mathematical expressions in TeX rather than approximating them in plain text.
> Inline formulas are delimited by `$...$` or `\(...\)`, displayed formulas by `$$...$$` or `\[...\]`;
> both are rendered in the browser by KaTeX. Write `$R/I$`, `$x^2$` and `$\mathbb{Z}/p$` rather than
> `R/I`, `x^2` and `ZZ/p` when you mean the mathematical object. The macros `\OO`, `\Hom`, `\End`,
> `\Ext` and `\Tor` are predefined.

> Distinguish *Macaulay2* syntax from mathematics. A symbol you would type at the prompt is code; the
> same symbol in the surrounding mathematics is math, and should be set in TeX. Mathematics is not
> rendered inside example blocks, so a formula written there will appear literally.

The delimiters and macros named are the four pairs and five macros `m2/html.m2` configures for
KaTeX's auto-render; `pre` and `code` are in its `ignoredTags`, which is where the last sentence
comes from.

one-off audit to produce a worklist rather than a check to keep, and a grep over the packages will
do it.
