Confirmed on 1.26.06, and worth adding what `#` on a `Matrix` actually returns: the number of
hash-table keys, which is **5 for every matrix**. So `Code.m2:888` builds its ring with exactly
five variables no matter what the ideal is, and `scarfChainComplex MonomialIdeal` fails for any
monomial ideal with more than five minimal generators:

```m2
i4 : numcols mingens I

o4 = 6

i5 : # (mingens I)

o5 = 5

i6 : scarfChainComplex I
stdio:6:17:(3):[1]: error: index 5 out of bounds 0 .. 4

i7 : scarfChainComplex first entries mingens I

      1      6      12      10      3
o7 = R  <-- R  <-- R   <-- R   <-- R
                                    
     0      1      2       3       4
```

That last line is a workaround in the meantime: the `List` overload sizes its ring from `#L`
correctly and is unaffected.

Below six generators the answer is right rather than merely lucky — `A` is only ever indexed at
`A_(vertexLabel m)` for `vertexLabel` in `0 .. n-1`, so the surplus variables are never touched. I
checked 3, 4 and 5 generator ideals and the two overloads agree on the complex and on every
differential. So the fix you propose, `numcols mingens I`, changes nothing below six and repairs
everything above it.
