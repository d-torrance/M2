Closing: your proposal 1 landed — in your own
[PR #3042](https://github.com/Macaulay2/M2/pull/3042) (`f69e7faf01`, merged 2023-12-30), two weeks
after you filed this. `Hom` now takes `MinimalGenerators` and trims only when it is set:

```m2
Hom = method(Options => {
	DegreeLimit       => null,
	MinimalGenerators => true,
	Strategy          => null,
	})
```

One difference from what you proposed, worth stating since it is the whole practical effect: you
suggested *"perhaps set to false by default"*, and it defaults to **true**. So the trim you measured
at 167s against 0.05s for the untrimmed kernel still happens unless a caller asks otherwise.

Checked that the option really skips the trim rather than being ignored — over `ZZ/32003[a..d]`
with `M = R^{0,-1,5:-1,6:-2}` and `N = coker random(R^3, R^{4:-2})`:

```
Hom(M, N)                              0.0146s
Hom(M, N, MinimalGenerators => false)  0.000026s
```

with `numgens` 39 either way, which is your complaint in miniature — nothing to trim, and almost all
the time spent trimming.

Proposal 2 (never trim) was not taken, and the aside about `prune` and `trim` preserving cached
`homomorphism`, `formation` and `components` is only partly served here: `Hom` sets
`cache.homomorphism` and `cache.formation` after the trim, but the general question about `prune`
and `trim` is untouched and would want its own issue.
