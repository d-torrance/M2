Measured today, and the estimate holds — with one correction to the base.

The rendered page carries 346 author links, but only **235 are `http(s)`**. The other 111 are
`mailto:`, which `ov_packages.m2:60-62` falls back to when an author gives no `HomePage`, and which
cannot be tested. Of the 235, checked one at a time with a browser user-agent and redirects
followed: **157 load and 58 are broken**; 18 more answer 403 or 999, which is bot-blocking and
almost certainly fine in a browser, and 2 timed out inconclusively. The 58 are 38 gone (37×404, one
410), 10 dead at the network layer — nine with no DNS record at all — 4 whose certificates do not
validate, and 6 with redirect faults.

So "about 60 / 340" was right on the count and off on the denominator: it is 58 of 235, a quarter of
the testable links, and it has not decayed in two years.

**One of the dead ones is in Core.** `m2/Core.m2:15` gives
`HomePage => "http://www.math.uiuc.edu/~dan/"`, which 301s to `https://math.illinois.edu~dan/` —
the slash is missing from the redirect — and does not resolve.

There is a third option besides not linking them, which is to **detect them**. Nothing in the tree
can: `Macaulay2/html-check-links/html-check-links.c` is 151 lines with no socket, no `curl` and no
`getaddrinfo` in it, so it only ever sees local anchors, and an external 404 has never been
checkable by anything we ship. A packaged checker does both halves at once:

```
$ linkchecker --check-extern --recursion-level=1 <the authors page>
That's it. 363 links in 494 URLs checked. 42 warnings found (95 ignored or duplicates not printed).
69 errors found.
Stopped checking at 2026-08-21 09:22:43-004 (1 minute, 47 seconds)
```

That is the whole page, `mailto:` entries included, in under two minutes. (69 against my 58 because
LinkChecker counts the 403s as errors and I did not.) #953 arrived at the same tool from the other
direction — it is in Debian stable, testing and unstable on every release architecture.
