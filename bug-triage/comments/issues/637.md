### This was really settled in 2017

Worth saying plainly, because it means the issue has been open nine years without anything
outstanding: @DanGrayson could not reproduce it on 1.9.2 or 1.10 the day it was filed, and
@antonleykin — who filed it on Yairon Cid Ruiz's behalf — confirmed the next day:

> I confirm habanero's 1.9.2 does not work for this... but 1.9.2.1 on my machine (close to
> current master) does give the same result as Dan's.
>
> I am not sure what "fixed" the issue -- the last minor change to that code was in 2015.
> Perhaps the new way promotion/lifting are done?

So the failure was already confined to a stale build — one laptop and the habanero server,
both on 1.9.2 — while current master was fine. It was resolved on arrival and simply never
closed.

### On attribution

I could not identify what changed either, and I am not going to guess: the window is between
1.9.2 and 1.9.2.1 in 2017, @antonleykin's own suggestion (the promote/lift rework) is a
guess, and he was right that `bFunction.module.m2` had not been touched since 2015.

One thing found while looking, recorded because it is adjacent rather than because it is the
answer: `bFunction(Module, List, List)` — this exact method — did get bug fixes later, in
`98de48e566` by Avi Steiner, merged in **#2836** ("The Great Dmodules Schism", 2023-05-17).
Among them a guard on a degenerate input:

```m2
+	  -- Make sure we don't get the 0 ideal in ZZ
+	  if zero I then I = ideal 0_(ring NM);
```

That postdates this issue going quiet by six years, so it is not the fix; it does suggest the
method had rough edges of the general kind reported here.
