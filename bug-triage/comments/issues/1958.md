Answering the question from March 2021 — *"What makes `QQ[x]` different from all these other rings?"* —
because monic normalization is only half of it. `gcd` on `QQ` never reaches the engine at all.

`m2/integers.m2` defines it as a content gcd over the rationals: clear both denominators to a common
`d`, take the integer gcd of the numerators, divide back. So `gcd(2_QQ, 4_QQ)` is 2 by construction,
and `QQ` agrees with `ZZ` for much the same reason `ZZ[x]` keeps its content — nothing on that path
uses the fact that `QQ` is a field. `QQ[x]` is the only one of the four that goes through
`rawGCDRingElement`, and there 2 and 8 are units, so the gcd is 1. Your `{2, 2, 2, 1}` is those two
implementations side by side rather than one rule applied inconsistently.

Both are deliberate, so there is nothing to fix in `rawGCDRingElement`. The two things you asked for
after that are still open, though: the `gcd` documentation says only "the greatest common divisor of
the arguments", with nothing about normalization in either case, and `liftUp matrix{{1/2*x},
{1/2*x}}` still returns `2x`.
