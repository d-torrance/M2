It allocates a zero matrix of the right shape and returns it without ever applying the map. That
accounts for every observation in this thread: the answer is `0` regardless of the ring, and it is
`0` rather than an error, so nothing warns you.

It goes back further than the report. The function arrived in @mikestillman's cc2940453d,
2014-06-14, titled *"Adding promote, lift, ring map eval for mutable matrices, part 1"* — and part 2
never followed, so this has been returning zeros for a little over twelve years, nine of them before
you filed.

Two things I checked while confirming it: dispatch is fine, `(symbol SPACE, RingMap, MutableMatrix)`
is defined at `ringmap.m2:189` and does get called; and the immutable path alongside it is correct,
so on 1.26.06 over `ZZ/11` with `h = map(R, R, {z^2})` you get `h z == z^2` and
`h matrix{{z}} == matrix{{z^2}}` while `h mutableMatrix{{z}}` is still `0`.
