Confirmed on 1.26.06, and the shift is systematic rather than specific to edges — `polyhedra(k, PC)`
returns the **(k−1)**-dimensional faces for every `k`.

The cause is at `Polyhedra/core/polyhedralComplex/methods.m2:5-13`:

```m2
   L := getProperty(PC, computedFacesThroughRays);
   dk := dim(PC) - k;
   if L#?dk then return L#dk
```

`L` is not keyed by `dim(PC) - d`. For `PC = polyhedralComplex hypercube 3` it has keys
`{-1, 0, 1, 2, 3}` with sizes `{1, 6, 12, 8, 1}` — the squares, edges, vertices and the cube itself
at `-1` — so it is keyed by `dim(PC) - d - 1`. Hence the off-by-one, and hence

```m2
i5 : polyhedra(0, PC)

o5 = {({}, {})}
```

the **empty face**, where the eight vertices were wanted.

Evaluating `L#(dim PC - k - 1)` by hand gives the right answer across the whole range, including
`k = dim PC` landing on `L#(-1)`:

| complex | `polyhedra(k)` now, k = 0.. | with `dim PC - k - 1` | correct f-vector |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| triangle | 1, 3, 3 | 3, 3, 1 | 3 vertices, 3 edges, 1 triangle |
| triangle ∪ segment (not pure) | 1, 4, 4 | 4, 4, 1 | 4 vertices, 4 edges, 1 triangle |
| `hypercube 3` | 1, 8, 12, 6 | 8, 12, 6, 1 | 8, 12, 6, 1 |
| `hypercube 4` | 1, 16, 32, 24, 8 | 16, 32, 24, 8, 1 | 16, 32, 24, 8, 1 |

The non-pure complex is included because that is where a dimension/codimension mix-up would be most
likely to survive a fix; it does not.

So `dk := dim(PC) - k - 1` looks right, and the method's own comment — *"Giving the k dimensional
Polyhedra of the Polyhedral Complex"* — says that is the intent. **I have not run the package's
test suite against that change**, only evaluated the corrected index by hand as above.
