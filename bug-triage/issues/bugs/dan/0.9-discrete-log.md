### What is being asked

The two forms are different questions and both are wanted: `log(a^99)` is the discrete logarithm with
respect to the field's own generator, and `log(a^2, a^99)` is the logarithm to an arbitrary base. The
engine already answers the first; the second is derivable from it.

### Why this one is cheap

The computation exists and is already exposed to the interpreter — this is a front-end method
installation rather than new mathematics, which puts it in a different class from most requests of this
kind. The decision worth making explicitly is what should happen for an element that is not a power of
the base, since the discrete logarithm then does not exist.
